### Diagnosis (vercel.json)
The `redirects` array (51 entries) is being ignored because `vercel.json` also defines a `routes` block. Per Vercel docs, presence of `routes` disables `redirects`/`rewrites`/`cleanUrls`. The catch-all `{ "src": "/(.*)", "dest": "/404.html", "status": 404 }` in the `routes` block then 404s everything that isn't a literal filesystem `.html` hit.

### Google index sanity check
`site:premierjointcare.com` returns: `/`, `/about-premier-rheumatology/`, `/contact-us/`, `/our-team/`. Three of those are 404 URLs from the legacy WordPress site. The current site's main pages and 45 condition pages are NOT in the Google index. This is the on-site cause of zero non-brand keyword rankings.
